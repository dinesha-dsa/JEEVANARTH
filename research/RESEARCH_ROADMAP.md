# JEEVANARTH Research Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Baseline freeze
**Status:** v1.0.0 public research preview released.

Actions:
- preserve source/tag;
- freeze documentation;
- reconcile equations with source code;
- document exact defaults and scoring rules.

## Phase 2 — Computational verification
- independent formula tests;
- boundary tests;
- scenario consistency;
- browser/device testing;
- bilingual terminology review.

**Gate:** no known material calculation defects.

## Phase 3 — Prior-art and literature review
- retirement/life-cycle models;
- financial well-being/resilience indices;
- healthy ageing and social-support frameworks;
- human-capital/second-career literature;
- explainable DSS;
- comparable public tools and patents.

**Output:** evidence-backed prior-art matrix and research-gap statement.

## Phase 4 — Pilot usability protocol
Design:
- participant criteria;
- consent/information sheet where applicable;
- structured feedback instrument;
- task scenarios;
- usability/clarity measures;
- qualitative feedback coding plan.

If intended for formal publication, confirm institutional ethics requirements before collecting human-subject research data.

## Phase 5 — Pilot study
Use a diverse sample across age, income, family structure, and financial-literacy levels. Avoid claiming population representativeness from a convenience pilot.

## Phase 6 — JLI/MEC refinement
Use expert review, sensitivity analysis, pilot evidence, and literature to revise:
- domains;
- weights;
- normalization;
- thresholds;
- intervention definitions.

## Phase 7 — v1.1.0
Implement only documented changes. Update:
- source;
- equations;
- assumptions;
- validation tests;
- changelog;
- release notes.

## Phase 8 — Advanced research
Candidate directions:
- Monte Carlo / stochastic returns;
- sequence-of-returns risk;
- mortality/longevity distributions;
- probabilistic goal success;
- tax-aware modelling;
- healthcare shock scenarios;
- multi-goal optimization;
- explainability experiments;
- longitudinal user studies.

## Phase 9 — Publication
Potential paper structure:
1. problem statement;
2. literature/research gap;
3. system architecture;
4. mathematical model;
5. JLI/MEC constructs;
6. verification;
7. pilot methodology;
8. results;
9. limitations;
10. future work.

Avoid novelty, validation, or effectiveness claims beyond the collected evidence.
