Method & limitations

JEEVANARTH uses deterministic cash-flow projection, inflation, annual investment accumulation/decumulation, loan amortisation, goal inflation, scenario sensitivity and rule-based guidance. Market returns, inflation, income, health costs and longevity are uncertain. Tax rules and product-specific regulations are not modelled in this release. Review important decisions with appropriately qualified professionals.
